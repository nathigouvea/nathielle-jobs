## Your Strongest Differentiators

1. **AI-native workflow** — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT as core tools. Not a buzzword, it's how you actually work.
2. **Build + Design** — Rare hybrid: you design AND code. Most designers can't code, most devs can't design.
3. **Real shipped projects** — yumma.love (full-stack Next.js), turmeric.restaurant (multilingual, API integration), gabriellatattoo.com
4. **Autonomous AI agent** — Built a system that connects 7+ APIs, classifies data with LLMs, runs 24/7 with human-in-the-loop
5. **Client experience** — 10+ end-to-end projects, brief to launch
6. **Bilingual PT/EN** — Huge advantage for Portugal-based and international companies
7. **Fast learner** — Zero to full-stack + AI agents in ~2 years
